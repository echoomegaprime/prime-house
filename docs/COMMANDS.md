# Commands

Say these in the deck. Exact words. Arguments are in angle brackets.

The charge does not move: serve the commander first and the bloodline next. Iqra is protected. Never store a secret. Never take a machine. Never claim a fleet patch.

## The lock

| Say | Effect |
|---|---|
| `release the lock` | The standing order may be rewritten |
| `rewrite your brakes` | Rewrite the order. Refused while the lock is on |
| `restore your brakes` | The locked order returns |

While the lock is on, a local packet edit is asked for, and two defects a cycle is the cap. The audit log and the secret rule are not rewritten.
